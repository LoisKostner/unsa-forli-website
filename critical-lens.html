The Critical Lens

UNSA's student magazine: student-led perspectives on the events shaping our international landscape.

Editorial mission

“In a time where we are encouraged to simplify narratives, this magazine aspires to challenge this dangerous assumption.”

The Critical Lens was born out of the desire to encourage students of the University of Bologna to share their perspectives on international affairs. It offers well-researched, nuanced essays — and a platform to strengthen academic writing and critical thinking outside the classroom.
