About our seminars

Learn by discussing

Our seminars are thematic meetings created by and for students: a topic is introduced, and then everyone takes part in the discussion.

Alongside them, we run interactive workshops with professors and guest speakers, workshops on negotiation and public speaking, and debates — to sharpen the skills that the MUN Club and the MUN Conferences put into practice.

Seminar · International Women's Day

Women in Diplomacy

Analyzing Women's Role and Participation in Today's International Affairs in the UN and Beyond

A space created by and for students to explore the role of women in international diplomacy, with particular attention to the historical, institutional and contemporary dynamics of the United Nations.

Gender-equal participation contributes to longer and more lasting peace after conflict. Yet, according to UN sources, between 1992 and 2018 women were only 13% of negotiators, 3% of mediators and 4% of signatories in major peace processes.
