What we do

Four ways to experience international affairs at the Forlì Campus — from the committee room to the dinner table.

I · Model United Nations

Diplomacy, simulated for real

In a Model United Nations, students act as delegates of UN Member States: they research a global issue, deliver speeches on behalf of their country, negotiate with other delegations and draft resolutions — just like real diplomats.

It is the heart of UNSA, and it works in two steps:

MUN ClubWeekly training to learn the rules and practise debating.Every Thursday MUN ConferenceA full day in a formal committee, from speeches to resolution.Our yearly simulation
