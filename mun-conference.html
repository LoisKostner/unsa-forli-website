RulesRoP

Rules of Procedure

Our Rules of Procedure (Second Edition) regulate every committee session. Here are the essentials.

Download the full RoP
Conduct & dress code
  • English is the only official language, for inclusivity.
  • Formal business attire; respectful traditional attire is welcome. No character portrayal or cultural appropriation.
  • Diplomatic decorum at all times: no form of discrimination is tolerated.
  • Attendance is mandatory; justified absences must be communicated to the Chairs in advance.
Technology & AI
  • Devices only at the Chairs' discretion — bring your materials printed.
  • AI is strictly prohibited during the simulation and for speeches and position papers.
  • Plagiarism leads to disciplinary measures.
Chairs, Staff & the Purple Point
  • The Chairs guide the sessions and manage the Speakers' List, points and motions.
  • The Staff assist delegates with notes, questions and logistics.
  • The Purple Point is a staff member dedicated to delegates' wellbeing.
Debate, points & motions
  • The General Speakers' List is the default mode; opening statements last up to 1'30".
  • Points of Personal Privilege, Order and Information.
  • Moderated and unmoderated caucuses, Tour de Table, Consultation of the Whole and more.
Documents & voting
  • Working Paper → Draft Resolution (2–4 sponsors, at least 4 signatories) → Amendments → Resolution.
  • One delegate, one vote. Motions pass by simple majority; a resolution needs a qualified majority (2/3).
