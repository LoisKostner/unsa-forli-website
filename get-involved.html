FAQ

Good to know

Do I need to study Political Science?
No. UNSA is open to all students, regardless of their academic background and origin.
Do I need to speak Italian?
No. All our activities are held in English.
Member or volunteer: what's the difference?
Members take part in our initiatives and get reduced fees. Volunteers are members who also help organise UNSA's activities in one of our departments.
Can I write for The Critical Lens?
Yes — see the call for papers.
How do I stay updated?
Follow @unsaforli on Instagram and check our News & Events.
