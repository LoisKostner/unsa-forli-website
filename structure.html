Every committee is open to new volunteers. No previous experience is required — just motivation and curiosity.

CommitteeI

Treasury

Treasury manages the funds granted by the University, drafts the annual budget and coordinates all of the association's expenses.

  • Managing the funds granted by the University of Bologna
  • Drafting the annual budget
  • Coordinating and tracking all expenses

CommitteeIII

Academics

Academics runs the weekly MUN Club, coordinates our MUN Conferences, carries out research and organises panels and seminars with professors.

  • Running the weekly MUN Club
  • Coordinating the MUN Conferences, one every semester
  • Research on committees and topics
  • Panels and seminars with professors, such as the Human Rights Day

Editorial Committee

Alongside the committees, the Editorial Committee produces The Critical Lens, UNSA's student magazine.

  • Calls for papers and selection of essays
  • Editing and proofreading
  • In-house reports
  • Layout, graphics and illustration
