Find your place

Every department is open to new volunteers. No previous experience is required — just motivation and curiosity.

DepartmentI

MUN Department

The heart of UNSA. We design and run our Model United Nations activities: the weekly MUN Club and the MUN Conference.

  • Planning the MUN Club programme and sessions
  • Choosing committees and topics, writing study guides
  • Training and appointing the Chairs
  • Keeping the Rules of Procedure up to date
  • Conference logistics, staff and the Purple Point

Editorial — The Critical Lens

We publish The Critical Lens, UNSA's student magazine: a platform for well-researched essays on international affairs.

  • Calls for papers and selection of essays
  • Editing and proofreading
  • In-house reports
  • Layout, graphics and illustration
