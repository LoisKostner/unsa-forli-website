## 2. Chi siamo (testi ufficiali)

### 2.1 Versione breve — "Who are we?" [September posts]
> We are an International Student Association of the University of Bologna based on the Forlì Campus.
>
> Our shared goal is to bring the Model United Nations (MUN) experience to our student community while promoting diversity and youth empowerment.
>
> We believe in offering accessible opportunities for all interested students, regardless of their academic background and origin, to develop hard and soft skills that enrich our journeys as global citizens.

### 2.2 Versione estesa [Critical Lens, Vol. 1 — firmata dalle co-presidenti]
> UNSA is the United Nations Student Association of the University of Bologna, located in the Forlì Campus. We were founded in 2024, with the aim of bringing high quality academic extracurricular activities for students, regardless of their background, to enrich their university journeys. Our main projects within our community involve MUN (Model United Nations) conferences, preparatory meetings, panel discussions featuring guests and esteemed members of our faculty, debate sessions, workshops, and various social gatherings and events to promote and enhance the multicultural nature of our student body.
>
> UNSA also stands as a platform for student engagement and empowerment, counting with 50 active volunteers, and increasing opportunities and potential for learning and growth. Our team works hard to bring to life new projects that bridge academic excellence and innovation.

### 2.3 Valori / pilastri
- **Diversity & inclusion** — definiti esplicitamente "UNSA's pillars" [Rules of Procedure, art. 4.2]

- **Accessibilità**: opportunità aperte a tutti, indipendentemente da percorso di studi e provenienza [September posts]
- **Crescita di hard e soft skills**, cittadinanza globale [September posts]

## 5. MUN Club (attività settimanale)

### 5.1 Descrizione [MUN CLUB STORIES]
> The MUN Club (Model United Nations) introduces students to international diplomacy through UN simulations. Members represent countries, debate global issues, and develop negotiation and public speaking skills.
>
> Throughout the program, participants learn how to research international topics, build strong arguments, and collaborate effectively within a formal committee environment.
>
> The program culminates in a final simulation, where everything learned is put into practice in a formal committee setting.

### 6.2 Regole di procedura (Rules of Procedure, Second Edition) — sintesi per il sito
Documento completo disponibile come PDF: si consiglia di pubblicarlo come download ("Rules of Procedure – MUN, Second Edition").

**Disposizioni generali**
- Lingua ufficiale: **inglese** (altre lingue non permesse, per inclusività).
- **Dress code:** business formale; abiti tradizionali ammessi se rispettosi della cultura del paese; vietati character portrayal e appropriazione culturale.
- **Presenza** obbligatoria; assenze giustificate da comunicare in anticipo a Chairs/tutor.
- **Condotta:** decoro diplomatico; nessuna forma di discriminazione tollerata (diversity & inclusion sono pilastri UNSA).
- **Tecnologia e AI:** dispositivi solo a discrezione dei Chairs (consigliato portare materiali stampati); **uso di AI vietato** durante la simulazione e nella scrittura di discorsi/position paper; plagio sanzionato.
- **Chairs:** guidano le sessioni, gestiscono Speakers' List, mozioni, diritti di parola.
- **Staff:** assistono i delegati (ushers), logistica. **Purple Point:** membro dello staff dedicato al benessere dei delegati.

**Linee guida per i delegati**
- Badge sempre visibile; si parla in terza persona a nome del proprio Stato; puntualità.
- Roll call: "Present" o "Present and Voting" (quest'ultimo non consente l'astensione).
- Dibattito formale = moderato dai Chairs; informale = unmoderated caucus.

**Procedura (in breve)**
- General Speakers' List (modalità di default); opening statements max 1'30"; yields (a un delegato, alle domande, ai Chairs); Right of Reply (30").
- Points: Personal Privilege, Order, Information.
- Motions: Moderated Caucus, Unmoderated Caucus, Extend Previous Caucus, Tour de Table, Consultation of the Whole (anche "ping-pong style"), Appeal the Decision of the Chairs, Suspension, Adjournment, Modify Speakers' Time. Maggioranza semplice (50%+1) salvo diversa indicazione.
- Documenti: Working Paper → Draft Resolution (min. 2 / max. 4 sponsor, min. 4 signatories) → Amendments (friendly / unfriendly) → Resolution.
- Voto: un voto per delegato; maggioranza semplice e qualificata; Roll Call Vote; Vote Clause by Clause; Divide the Question.

⚠️ Nel PDF alcune frazioni non sono estraibili come testo (quorum di apertura, maggioranza qualificata, voti necessari per approvare una risoluzione — probabilmente "2/3"): da verificare sul documento originale.
