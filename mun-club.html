About the Club

Learn diplomacy by doing it

The MUN Club introduces students to international diplomacy through UN simulations. Members represent countries, debate global issues, and develop negotiation and public speaking skills.

Throughout the programme, participants learn how to research international topics, build strong arguments and collaborate effectively within a formal committee environment. No previous experience is needed.
