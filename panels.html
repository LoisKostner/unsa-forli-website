About our panels

Meet the people who study the world

Our panel discussions bring professors of the University of Bologna, international guest speakers and partner organisations to the Forlì Campus.

Each panel is organised by our Academics committee and is open to all students. It is a chance to hear from those who research, negotiate and report on international affairs, and to ask them your own questions.
